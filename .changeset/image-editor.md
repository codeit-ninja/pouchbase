Add an image editor for PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP files: crop, rotate, flip, resize, color adjustments (brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, grayscale, sepia) with presets, and a choice of output format and quality. Edits are applied on the server with Photon (WebAssembly) and replace the original; thumbnails are regenerated.
